Source: World Bank, Remittance Prices Worldwide (RPW), dataset 2011 to Q3 2025, sheet “Dataset (from Q2 2016)”. License CC BY 4.0.

What it measures: each observation is a price quote for one service (provider × corridor × payment and pickup method) for a hypothetical USD 200 and USD 500 transfer, collected by mystery shopping. These are not actual transactions and are not volume-weighted.

Method: simple average of total cost (fee + FX margin) across transparent services, as in the World Bank global average (SDG indicator 10.c.1). With no filters, the series reproduces the published global average (for example, 6.36% in Q3 2025).

Regions and status: UN World Economic Situation and Prospects (WESP) Annex classification: region, subregion and development status (developed, economies in transition, developing). Where a country has no subregion, its region is used. Kosovo and Tonga are not in the classification and show as “Unclassified”.

Service fields: receiving network coverage (High, Medium, Low) is the World Bank's rating of how extensive the provider's pickup network is in the receiving country. Pickup method is how the money reaches the recipient (cash, bank account, mobile wallet). Pickup location is where cash is collected (agent, bank branch, home delivery); it is blank for most account and wallet deposits and shows as “Not specified”. “Own/partner bank” is a deposit to an account at the provider’s own or partner bank. Spelling variants were merged.

Cleaning: in the original file, values for 2024 Q3 and Q4 were stored as text; they were converted to numbers. The World Bank published no data for Q2 2025.
